Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Light Peace Academy admissions and programs.

How do I apply for admission?
You can apply online through our admissions portal or visit our campus to pick up an application form. Our admissions team will guide you through the entire process.
What documents do I need for admission?
Required documents include birth certificate, previous school records, medical records, and passport-sized photos. International students may need additional documentation.
Are there scholarships available?
Yes, we offer merit-based and need-based scholarships. Contact our admissions office to learn about eligibility criteria and application deadlines.
Can I schedule a campus tour?
Absolutely! We encourage prospective students and families to visit our campus. Tours are available Monday through Saturday. Please call ahead to schedule your visit.
