📚 Our Rich History

30 Years of Excellence

From humble beginnings to becoming a beacon of educational excellence, discover the transformative journey that has shaped Light Peace Academy into what it is today.

Our Journey Through Time

Explore the key milestones that have defined our path to becoming a leading educational institution.

1995 - The Beginning

Light Peace Academy was founded with a vision to provide quality education. Starting with dedicated teachers, we began our mission to transform lives through education.

2005 - Growth & Expansion

A decade of remarkable growth saw our enrollment double and our facilities expand. We introduced modern teaching methodologies and established partnerships with international educational institutions.

2015 - Digital Transformation

Embracing technology, we integrated digital learning tools, established computer labs, and launched our first online learning platform to prepare students for the digital age.

2025 - Excellence Today

Today, Light Peace Academy stands as a beacon of educational excellence, having empowered thousands of students with a strong commitment to holistic development that continues to shape future leaders.
