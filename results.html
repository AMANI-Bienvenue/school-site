Grade Reports

At Light Peace Academy, we provide detailed grade reports to help students and parents track academic progress. Our grading system is based on continuous assessment, including assignments, quizzes, mid-term exams, and final examinations.

Grading Scale

Grade Percentage Description
A 90-100% Excellent
B 80-89% Very Good
C 70-79% Good
D 60-69% Satisfactory
F Below 60% Fail

Progress Tracking

We encourage regular communication between teachers, students, and parents. Progress reports are issued quarterly, and parent-teacher conferences are held to discuss individual student performance and areas for improvement.

Accessing Results

Results are available through our secure online portal. Parents and students can log in to view detailed reports, including subject-wise breakdowns and comments from teachers. For any questions regarding results, please contact the academic office.
